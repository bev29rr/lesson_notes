Define Archimedes' Principle→The upthrust of an object submerged (in a fluid) is equal to the weight of the fluid it displaces
Define Centre of Mass→The SINGLE POINT through which the object's mass can be said to act
Define Centre of Gravity→The SINGLE POINT through which the object's weight can be said to act
Define Couple→Two equal and opposite parallel forces (that act on an object through different lines of action). There is no resultant force but there is rotation.
Define Equilibrium→When the resultant force and the resultant momentum are zero.
Define Principle of Moments→∑ anticlockwise moments = ∑ clockwise moments

Define a Brittle Material→An object that shows little strain before reaching its fracture point
Define a Ductile Material→An object that can undergo large strain (extension) before reaching its fracture point
State the three main points on a Stress-Strain Graph
    Yield point (proportional limit)
    UTS (ultimate tensille strength)
    Breaking/fracture point
