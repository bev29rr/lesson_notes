State Coulomb's Law→The ELECTROSTATIC FORCE between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of their charges and inversely propertional to the square of their separation. (F=Qq/kr^2)
Define Electric Potential and Electric Potential Energy
    V: Work Done per unit charge to bring a POSITIVE charge FROM infinity TO that point (in the field)
    U (Energy): Work Done to bring a POSITIVE charge FROM infinity TO that point (in the field)
Define Electric Field→A region surrrounding a charged object (which causes a force to be exerted on it)
Define Permitivity→The meaterial's ability to PERMIT an ELECTRIC FIELD
Define Permeability→A material's ability to SUPPORT the FORMATION of a MAGNETIC FIELD 

State Faraday's Law→The magnitude of the induced EMF is directly proportional to the rate of change of MAGNETIC FLUX LINKAGE
State the direction of Magnetic Field Lines (in terms of force experienced)
    North to South
    (direction of the force on a MOVING positive charge)
Lenz's Law→The DIRECTION of an INDUCED CURRENT is such that it OPPOSES the current that created it

State the difference between Activity and Count Rate
    Activity: The rate of decay of an isotope
    Count Rate: The MEASURED rate of decay

    ⇒ Slow down neutrons (to make fission more likely)
    Control Rods
    ⇒ Absorb neutrons and control the rate of reaction
