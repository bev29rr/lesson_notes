Define Internal Energy→Sum of the kinetic energies and potential energies of particles in a given system
Define Specific Heat Capacity→The temperature required to raise 1 kg of a substance by 1 K
Define (Specific) Latent Heat→The temperature required to change the state (of 1kg) of a substance WITHOUT changing temperature
State the Ideal Gas conditions
    Particles have negligible volume – The gas molecules are so small that their own volume can be ignored compared to the container’s volume.
    No intermolecular forces – There are no attractions or repulsions between particles.
    Elastic collisions – Collisions between gas particles and with container walls are perfectly elastic (no energy loss).
    Constant, random motion – Gas particles move in continuous, random straight-line motion.
    Temperature is proportional to kinetic energy – The average kinetic energy of particles depends only on the absolute temperature (in kelvins).

Define Damping and state Types of Damping (with a short description)
    Definition: The DISSIPATION OF ENERGY, causing the amplitude to decrease
    Overdamping: It takes a long time to return to the EQUILIBRIUM position
    Critical Damping: It restores the EQUILIBRIUM position without oscillating
    Underdamping: Energy is gradually released, oscillating through the EQUILIBRIUM

Define Absorption and Emmision spectrum
    Absorption: Dark lines caused when a COOLER gas absorbs specific wavelengths from the CONTINUOUS spectrum
    Emmision: Bright lines caused when a HOTTER gas emits specific wavelengths
