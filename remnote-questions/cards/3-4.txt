Difference between EMF and Voltage
    EMF: the energy SUPPLIED per unit charge FROM A SOURCE
    Voltage: the energy USED per unit charge BETWEEN TWO POINTS
Define Coherence→waves with the same FREQUENCY and CONSTANT phase difference

Define Longitudinal Waves and Transverse Waves
    Longitudinal: Oscillations parallel to the DIRECTION OF ENERGY TRANSFER
    Transverse: Oscillations perpendicular to the DIRECTION OF ENERGY TRANSFER
Compare Progressive Wave vs Stationary Wave
    Progressive: Transfers energy (from one point to another)
    Stationary: Doesn't transfer (but stores) energy
Define Phase Difference and Path Difference→The difference in radians/distance between two points on a wave
Define Polarisation→When a wave is restricted to oscillate in a single plane
